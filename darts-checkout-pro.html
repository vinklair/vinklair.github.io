Support

Questions

How strong is the computer?

Pick a level, from pub player (a 45 average) to pro (95), or fine-tune its three-dart average. It aims at real routes and misses like a person would, so its checkouts come and go too.

Can I continue a match later?

Yes. Every dart is saved as you enter it. The Scorer tab shows your unfinished matches at the top; tap one to carry on.

Do my custom checkouts and badges sync?

Yes. They are stored in your own iCloud account and appear on your other devices signed in to it and on your Apple Watch. Nobody else, including me, can see them.

A checkout route looks wrong to me.

Press and hold it to add the route you prefer, hide the one you don't like, or tag it. And please tell me through the feedback form in the Info tab: the default routes get better with every report.

Still stuck?
Write to vinklair.apps@gmail.com, or use the feedback form in the app's Info tab.
