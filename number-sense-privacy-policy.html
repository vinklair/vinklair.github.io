Number Sense privacy policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

In short

  • Number Sense collects no data at all.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking and no accounts.
  • Your settings, favourite languages and streaks stay on your device.

Who is responsible

Number Sense - Hearing ("the app") is made by Vinklair Apps, an independent developer ("I", "me"). For any privacy question, write to vinklair.apps@gmail.com.

No data collection

The app does not collect, store or send any personal information. You never need to give your name, email address or any other details to use every feature.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising frameworks. What you do in the app is not recorded or monitored by me or by anyone else, and the app never asks to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Data on your device

Your settings, number ranges, favourite languages and practice streaks are stored on your device only. They leave it only if you share them yourself, for example in a screenshot, or as part of your own device backups. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

You buy the app through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and I never see your payment details.

Third parties

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to share or sell. No information is disclosed to advertisers, marketing partners or analytics providers.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date at the top.
