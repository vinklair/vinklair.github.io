Darts Checkout privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short

  • Your matches, scores and settings stay on your device.
  • Your custom checkout routes and badges sync through your own iCloud account. I can't see them.
  • There are no ads and no tracking. The Apple Watch app talks only to your iPhone.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) are sent only if you say yes, and never include your name.
  • Feedback you send from the app reaches me through iCloud, and holds only what you write.
  • There are no accounts, and I never sell your data.

Who is responsible

Darts Checkout (the free app, "the app") is made by Vinklair Apps, an independent developer ("I", "me"). For any privacy question, write to vinklair.apps@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

The app stores these on your iPhone or iPad only. None of it is sent to me or to anyone else:

  • Scorer matches: the player names you type, the darts you enter, and the match settings.
  • Game and training results: your best scores and levels in the games and board drills, and your hit rate on each double.
  • Settings: appearance, layout, font size, orientation and similar preferences.

Deleting the app deletes this data. Your device backups may still contain it, under your control.

Data synced through your iCloud account

Your checkout customizations (your own routes, hidden routes, route order and badges) are saved in iCloud key-value storage in your own Apple Account, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same account. Apple handles this storage under the Apple Privacy Policy. I have no access to it. You can change or remove any customization in the app at any time, and the change syncs to your other devices.

Usage statistics and crash reports, only with your yes

The first time you open version 6.0 or later, the app asks whether it may send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. Until you say yes, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics send nothing. You can change your answer any time in the Info tab, under Settings. With your yes, the app uses these Google Firebase services to understand how it is used and to fix problems:

  • Firebase Analytics records which screens and features are used, for example opening a tab, starting a game or a scorer match. It collects an app instance identifier, device and operating system details, app version and approximate location derived from the IP address. It also records each purchase you make in the app: which product, its price and the currency, never your payment details.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes, with technical details about the device and what the app was doing.

Firebase Remote Config downloads settings for the app, such as how often the review request appears, whatever your answer. It uses an installation identifier and sends nothing about how you use the app.

None of this includes your name, your email address, the player names you type or the darts you enter, and none of it is used for advertising. Google processes it on my behalf. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Feedback you send

The Info tab has a feedback form. Only when you send it, the app stores a report in the app's own iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit), where I read it: the reason you picked, the note you wrote, the date, which app and version, the iOS version and the device model. It holds no name or email address unless you write one into the note. Write to me and I will delete a report you sent.

No ads, no tracking

Since version 6.0 the app shows no ads and contains no advertising software. It never asks to track you, and it doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.

Purchases

You can buy Darts Checkout Pro or a Buy Me a Coffee tip in the app. Apple processes the payment. The app only receives confirmation of what you bought, so it can unlock Pro and restore the purchase on your other devices (an earlier Remove Ads purchase counts as Pro). I never see your payment details. If you said yes to usage statistics, Firebase Analytics also records the purchase: the product, its price and the currency.

What I don't do

  • I don't ask you to create an account.
  • I don't sell your data or share it for anyone else's marketing.
  • I don't collect your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or health data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, write to me and I will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Because I don't collect your name or contact details, most data can't be linked to you by me. Write to me and I will do what I can, including asking Google to delete analytics data for your app instance.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date at the top.
