Darts Checkout privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short

  • Your matches, scores and settings stay on your device.
  • Your custom checkout routes and badges sync through your own iCloud account. I can't see them.
  • There are no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics. The Apple Watch app talks only to your iPhone.
  • Once a day the app tells my server it was opened, with no identifier, so I know how many devices use it. It also asks whether a newer version is out.
  • Feedback you send from the app reaches me through iCloud, and holds only what you write.
  • There are no accounts, and I never sell your data.

Who is responsible

Darts Checkout (the free app, "the app") is made by Vinklair Apps, an independent developer ("I", "me"). For any privacy question, write to vinklair.apps@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

The app stores these on your iPhone or iPad only. None of it is sent to me or to anyone else:

  • Scorer matches: the player names you type, the darts you enter, and the match settings.
  • Game and training results: your best scores and levels in the games and board drills, and your hit rate on each double.
  • Settings: appearance, layout, font size, orientation and similar preferences.
  • Counters: the day the app was last counted (see below), and how many days you used the app and how many good moments it saw, such as a match won or a drill finished, so it can ask for a rating at a good time. The rating prompt is Apple's own: Apple decides whether it appears, and your answer goes to Apple, not to me.

Deleting the app deletes this data. Your device backups may still contain it, under your control.

Data synced through your iCloud account

Your checkout customizations (your own routes, hidden routes, route order and badges) are saved in iCloud key-value storage in your own Apple Account, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same account. Apple handles this storage under the Apple Privacy Policy. I have no access to it. You can change or remove any customization in the app at any time, and the change syncs to your other devices.

The daily count and the update check

Since version 6.1 the app contains no third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising software. It talks to my own small server at api.vinklairapps.com for two things only:

  • The daily count. The first time you open the app on a day (in UTC), it sends one request that says only "the free Darts Checkout app was opened". It carries no identifier, no cookie and nothing about you, your device or how you use the app. The server adds one to that day's number and keeps nothing else, so I see how many devices used the app each day, never which ones. On your device the app remembers only the day of the last count.
  • The update check. When you open the app, at most every few hours, it asks the server which version is the newest. The app compares it with its own version on your device, and the request counts nothing. If a newer version is out, the app tells you. If your version is too old to keep working properly, it asks you to update before you go on.

Neither request includes your name, your email address, the player names you type or the darts you enter. The server runs on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver the request, as for any website you visit, and which I use as my processor. The server doesn't store your IP address or any request header, and its logs are turned off. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Version 6.0 and Firebase

Version 6.0 asked whether it may send usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase, and sent them only with your yes: Firebase Analytics recorded the screens and features used and your purchases, with an app instance identifier, device details and approximate location from the IP address, and Firebase Crashlytics sent crash reports. Firebase Remote Config downloaded settings for the app whatever your answer. Version 6.1 removed Firebase completely, so updating stops all of it. What version 6.0 sent stays with Google, which processed it on my behalf, until it expires or I delete it. Write to me if you want it deleted sooner. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Feedback you send

The Info tab has a feedback form. Only when you send it, the app stores a report in the app's own iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit), where I read it: the reason you picked, the note you wrote, the date, which app and version, the iOS version and the device model. It holds no name or email address unless you write one into the note. Write to me and I will delete a report you sent.

No ads, no tracking

Since version 6.0 the app shows no ads and contains no advertising software. It never asks to track you, and it doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.

Purchases

You can buy Darts Checkout Pro or a Buy Me a Coffee tip in the app. Apple processes the payment. The app only receives confirmation of what you bought, so it can unlock Pro and restore the purchase on your other devices (an earlier Remove Ads purchase counts as Pro). I never see your payment details.

What I don't do

  • I don't ask you to create an account.
  • I don't sell your data or share it for anyone else's marketing.
  • I don't collect your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or health data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, write to me and I will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Because I don't collect your name or contact details, and nothing the app sends identifies you, most data can't be linked to you by me. Write to me and I will do what I can, including deleting feedback you sent and the Firebase data from version 6.0.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date at the top.
