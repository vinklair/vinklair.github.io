Numbers by Ear 2.1 · coming in November 2026

Made for iPhone Duo, in every posture.

Closed, it is a phone. Open, it gains a sidebar. Half open like a laptop, a game lies under your hand. Half open like a book, practice and all ten games spread across two pages, with nothing on the fold.

Four ways to hold it

One app, laid out for each

Every screen of the app is laid out for iPhone Duo, not stretched to it: Learn, practice, Say it back, Stats, Settings and all ten games.

Closed

A phone on the outer display, with every game fitted to it, on its side too.

Open

The inner display gains a sidebar, and a session or a game uses the room it gives.

Half open, like a laptop

The keypad or the game lies on the lower half, under the hand: the digits to type, the street to tap. What you heard and the score stand above.

Half open, like a book

Two pages: the number you hear on one, the keypad on the other; a game's scene on one, its card or controls on the other.

Slides

As the device draws them

Taken in Device Hub from the app running on the iPhone Duo simulator. Each slide is 3840×2160; open one for the full size.

How it fits

The fold comes from the system

Nothing on the fold

Each layout reads the fold from the system's division region, in its own coordinates, and keeps the band clear. No screen size is hard-coded.

It moves with the hinge

Folding or unfolding animates each screen into two pages or back, and a game keeps its round as it goes.

Any iPhone on its side

The same two pages serve every iPhone turned on its side, and every iPad.

The system's own parts

The sidebar is the system's split view, the bars are SwiftUI's own, and Liquid Glass comes with them.

The app

Numbers by Ear

Learners who know every word of a language still miss a number said at speed: a price, a time, a phone number. Numbers by Ear speaks them in dozens of languages with the voices on the device. Learners type what they heard, or say it back and have it checked on the device, and stars from practice open ten games.

Version
2.1, coming in November 2026. Also new: try a locked game for two rounds, and choose the app's own language.
Devices
iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad, iOS 17 or later
Price
Free, with a one-time Pro unlock. No ads, no subscription.
Languages
Dozens to learn, through the device's own voices. The app itself in English and Hungarian.
Privacy
Speech is recognized on the device only. No analytics, no tracking, no account; progress syncs through the learner's own iCloud.
